The Root of the Problem

↓

Primary Problem Statement

Apartment residents struggle to get maintenance issues resolved efficiently because complaints are reported through fragmented communication channels, making it difficult to track requests, identify responsibility, and receive timely updates.

USER

Apartment residents

NEED

A clear, reliable way to report, track, and understand issue progress.

BECAUSE

Current communication lacks visible ownership, priority, and status.

Design Thinking Concepts Applied

In this UX case study and problem statement formulation, the primary Design Thinking phases utilized are Empathize and Define.

1. Empathize

Demonstrated by bringing Aarav Mehta to life and mapping his dynamic journey. We identified frustrations and behaviors of all stakeholders dynamically to truly understand user pain points.

2. Define

Synthesized our research to boldly articulate the Core Problem Statement right at the top of the page. We established the chain reaction of scattered communication to set a clear direction for Ideation.
