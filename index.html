01 The Approach

            
Before jumping to solutions, we must understand the people and define the real issue. This study applies two core Design Thinking phases.

            

                

                    
1. Empathize

                    
We start by mapping the user journey to truly feel the frustrations, scattered communications, and delays experienced by residents and staff alike.

                

                

                    
2. Define

                    
We synthesize that empathy into a crystal-clear problem statement, ensuring we are solving the root cause, not just the symptoms.

05 The Core Problem

            
            

                
The Root of the Problem

                
The visible complaint is usually: “my maintenance issue is taking too long.”

                

                    
Scattered communication

                    
↓

                    
incomplete / duplicate information

                    
↓

                    
unclear priority & ownership

                    
↓

                    
weak status visibility

                    
↓

                    
frustration for everyone

                

            


            

                
Primary Problem Statement

                
Apartment residents struggle to get maintenance issues resolved efficiently because complaints are reported through fragmented communication channels, making it difficult to track requests, identify responsibility, and receive timely updates.

                
                

                    

                        
USER

                        
Apartment residents

                    

                    

                        
NEED

                        
A clear, reliable way to report, track, and understand issue progress.

                    

                    

                        
BECAUSE

                        
Current communication lacks visible ownership, priority, and status.
